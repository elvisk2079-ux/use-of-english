How to work

  1. Do the exercise in the book with your teacher.
  2. At home, open its drill and go through all five parts.
  3. Watch the board at the top: five dots means you know the phrase. Red frames show what to repeat.
  4. Come back in two or three days and press “Start again” – the questions are shuffled each time.
